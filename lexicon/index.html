The CLEARED Lexicon

The words for the thing you are already doing

Three terms carry the CLEARED framework. Each one names a decision practitioners already make, usually without a name for it, and almost always without a record of it.

Term 01

Red/Yellow/Green Zone Model

A framework for sorting AI use in practice by risk exposure. Every tool in your practice sits in one of three zones, and the zone determines how much review the output requires before it moves.

  • Green ZoneAI use that does not touch clinical content or protected health information.
  • Yellow ZoneAI use that touches client-facing content without generating clinical recommendations.
  • Red ZoneAI use that intersects with PHI, clinical decisions, or content reaching clients without human review.

Strategic Health Group documented the framework first.

Term 02

Human Authority Filter

The principle that no AI output reaches a client without a credentialed human review checkpoint.

The Filter is not a tool. It is the practice you build around your AI use to keep the practitioner accountable for what reaches the client.

Term 03

Documentation Discipline

The practice of recording what came from the AI tool, what came from your training, and what synthesis happened in between.

It is the documentation standard that survives a five-year audit because it shows the human reasoning behind every output.
