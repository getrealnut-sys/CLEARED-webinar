Questions

What practitioners ask first

Who is CLEARED for?
Health and wellness practitioners using AI tools in client-facing work. Board-certified health coaches, integrative practitioners, NBHWC-certified coaches, and holistic clinicians who need a defensible governance practice that survives a regulatory audit.
What is the Red/Yellow/Green Zone Model?
A framework for sorting AI use in practice by risk exposure. Green Zone: AI use that does not touch clinical content or protected health information. Yellow Zone: AI use that touches client-facing content without generating clinical recommendations. Red Zone: AI use that intersects with PHI, clinical decisions, or content reaching clients without human review. Strategic Health Group documented the framework first.
What is the Human Authority Filter?
The principle that no AI output reaches a client without a credentialed human review checkpoint. The Filter is not a tool. It is the practice you build around your AI use to keep the practitioner accountable for what reaches the client.
What is Documentation Discipline?
The practice of recording what came from the AI tool, what came from your training, and what synthesis happened in between. It is the documentation standard that survives a five-year audit because it shows the human reasoning behind every output.
Do I need to disclose AI use to my clients?
Increasingly, yes. The question is no longer whether disclosure is good practice. It is whether your current documentation satisfies the legal standard where your clients are located. Several states, including Washington, Maine, Vermont, and soon Louisiana, have moved from voluntary to required disclosure. The CLEARED Intelligence Brief tracks state-by-state changes weekly.
What is the difference between the CLEARED Assessment and the AI Workflow Audit?
The Assessment ($97) is a written walk-through of where your current AI use sits on the Red/Yellow/Green Zone Model, delivered within 48 hours. The AI Workflow Audit ($297) is a 60-minute call covering one specific workflow end-to-end, with a documented governance recommendation you can act on.
Is CLEARED HIPAA training?
No. HIPAA training covers protected health information at the technical and operational level. CLEARED covers practitioner-level AI governance. How you decide whether an AI tool belongs in a workflow, how you document its use, and how you maintain the human authority filter. The two are complementary, not interchangeable.
Will my AI vendor's compliance posture cover me?
Vendor compliance covers the vendor. Practitioner responsibility for what the AI produces and how it reaches the client stays with the practitioner. A Business Associate Agreement with your transcription tool does not substitute for a documented governance practice on your side.
What if I do not currently use AI in my practice, does any of this apply?
If you do not use AI at all, the disclosure requirements do not apply to you. Audit requirements that flow from federal funding still do. And the practitioners around you are using AI, which shapes the standard your clients increasingly expect.
